# Census of Anti-Black Racism and Antisemitism Research

## Original production prompt

This prompt was used to request the census underlying `racism_antisemitism_top_journals_census_2000_2026.xlsx`. The requested coverage ends September 18, 2026. Every census sheet of that workbook is carried unchanged into this repository's source workbook, `data/source/racism_antisemitism_census_with_modern_figure_2.xlsx`, which adds only the `Figure 2` sheet. The census was run as a model-assisted ChatGPT conversation (GPT-5.6 Sol Light, per footnote 3 of the post); no executable collection code or full transcript was preserved.

> Conduct the complete census we discussed of research articles published from January 1, 2000 through September 18, 2026 in the top 10 journals in economics, political science, psychology, and sociology. Compare articles primarily about (1) anti-Black racism/discrimination and (2) antisemitism/anti-Jewish prejudice or discrimination.
>
> I want an article-level census, not an estimate. Establish and document a defensible top-10 journal list for each discipline. Search each journal systematically and retain every candidate article. Manually classify candidates as A = racism/antisemitism is a primary focus; B = substantial focus but part of a broader inquiry; C = incidental/not qualifying. Apply exactly symmetrical inclusion standards to the two subjects. Separately classify empirical versus theoretical/conceptual/review papers.
>
> Produce a master spreadsheet containing discipline, journal, year, authors, title, DOI/URL, subject, A/B/C classification, empirical/nonempirical status, population/country, and a short explanation for inclusion or exclusion. Report A-only totals, A+B totals, empirical A-only totals, results by discipline, journal and year, anti-Black/antisemitism ratios, and—if feasible—rates per 1,000 substantive articles published. Preserve borderline and excluded candidates so the coding is auditable.
>
> Do not use the earlier estimate of approximately 365 anti-Black papers and 17 antisemitism papers as evidence; treat it solely as a hypothesis to test. Continue the research until the 40-journal census is complete rather than stopping after producing a methodology or preliminary sample.

## Reuse note

Before rerunning the census, update the end date and specify whether the existing journal list and prior classifications should be preserved, independently audited, or rebuilt from scratch.
