# Prompt 1: build the journal census

Conduct a complete article-level census of research published from January 1, 2000 through September 18, 2026 in ten leading journals in economics, political science, psychology, and sociology. Compare papers primarily about (1) anti-Black racism or discrimination and (2) antisemitism or anti-Jewish prejudice and discrimination.

Establish and document a defensible ten-journal list for each discipline. Resolve journal identities and ISSN streams. Retrieve all journal-article metadata in the date window, remove duplicates and clearly non-substantive publication types, and retain every keyword-screened candidate for audit.

Apply symmetrical inclusion rules to the two subjects. Produce a record-level table containing discipline, journal, publication year, authors, title, DOI or URL, subject, relevance class, empirical status, population or country, matched terms, and a concise coding rationale. Preserve borderline and excluded records.

Do not treat earlier aggregate estimates as evidence. Continue until all forty journal streams have been checked through the cutoff date.
