The paper’s second embedded PNG is retained as the original Figure 2. The eight bar heights agree with the Figure 2 sheet’s X2:Z5 values. The source workbook is unchanged. CSVs preserve workbook values, including candidate exclusions and metadata gaps.

## Workbook protocol

See `data/codebook.csv` for the complete supplied rules, `data/journal_universe.csv` for all 40 journals and identifiers, and `data/search_log.csv` for the supplied retrieval description. Both subjects require a target group plus a discriminatory process; identity alone is insufficient. A means primary focus, B substantial focus within a broader inquiry, and C incidental/excluded. Empirical includes original quantitative, qualitative, experimental, computational, archival, historical-data or survey analysis. The supplied protocol codes reviews and meta-analyses as nonempirical.

Publication dates and observation dates are distinct. Figure 2’s modern criterion is at least one observation **after 1960**, not simply publication after 1960. The figure is not restricted to US populations. “Top 10” represents the workbook’s selected set, not a literal ranking order. Denominator article totals are metadata proxies and are not used for this figure.

## Missing modern inclusion ledger

The master has 17 columns, none identifying an observation year or modern inclusion decision. An exact empirical A-to-modern transition cannot therefore be recovered mechanically. The repository does not infer exclusions from titles or fabricate the missing ledger. A future audit should add article/subject ID, observation range, modern flag, supporting page citation, reviewer and adjudication status, and reconcile those records against Figure 2.
